GAME RULES & PERMISSIONS

V2.5
  • Turn-Based Typing Race: Players take turns typing randomly assigned words to advance across 50 tiles.
  • Speed Matters: Typing under 1.2s awards +8 tiles, under 2.5s gives +6 tiles, under 5.0s gives +4 tiles, and typos move you only +1 tile.
  • Patches & Bugs: Landing on green patches catapults you forward; stepping on yellow bugs sends you sliding backwards!
  • Energy & Knockbacks: Landing on an opponent knocks them back 4 tiles and steals 5 Energy points.
  • Stuck Loop Tiebreaker: If both players get stuck around the same tile for 3 turns, a rapid-fire Sudden Death Speed Round triggers!
